# Kaizen Operations Loop

Use for server, platform, security, observability, backup, recovery, and automation improvement.

Baseline toil, incident/change frequency, alert quality, restore time, failed-path behaviour, distro parity, and operator effort. Observe the system, not only the runbook. Use a small reversible change, test Debian-family and RHEL-family paths, verify idempotence and least privilege, exercise rollback/recovery, and record the result. Standardise only after evidence in scripts, references, tests, and operator documentation.

Facility Move contributes charter/readiness/cutover/stabilisation/closeout patterns. Platform Enterprise contributes sociotechnical ownership and internal-user feedback. Lean/Kaizen contributes waste removal and standard work. Current distro and security facts still require verification.
